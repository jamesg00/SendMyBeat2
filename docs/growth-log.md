# Growth log

One place to record what we try for growth and what it did, so we repeat what works and drop what doesn't. The plan itself lives in [GROWTH_PLAN.md](../GROWTH_PLAN.md); this file is the scoreboard.

How to use it:
- Add a line when you start something (a post, a page, a feature, a price change).
- Come back a week later and fill in the result, with numbers where you have them (signups, uploads, visits, where they came from).
- Newest entries go at the top.
